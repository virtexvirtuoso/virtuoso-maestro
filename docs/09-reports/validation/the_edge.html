Meta-Insight: "In Chop, Do Less"

All 4 agents independently converged on the same conclusion: the system doesn't need more signals for choppy markets — it needs less exposure. The edge comes from trends. In chop, the optimal action is to preserve capital.

Priority 1: Cash-When-Flat

Replace hedge with 100% cash when all directional signals are flat. Adds 0 parameters, removes a feature that lost money in chop. Expected to eliminate 2-4% of the -14.83% loss.

Priority 2: Vol-Target Sizing

Replace fixed vol_mult=2.0 with dynamic target_vol/realized_vol scaling. Auto-reduces size when vol spikes (chop = high vol), auto-increases in calm trends. Adds 1 parameter — needs WF validation.

Hard Skip: Mean-Reversion & Faster Stops

Mean-reversion contradicts the trend-following core. Faster stops are proven destroyers of trend-following returns through whipsaw. Both would fail walk-forward validation.
